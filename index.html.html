📚 Official Legal & Help Knowledge Base / आधिकारिक कानूनी एवं सहायता जानकारी
English — Official sources used for KAWACH:
• Child Helpline 1098: Under Mission Vatsalya, Child Helpline is coordinated with State/District functionaries and integrated with ERSS-112. The official Ministry page states that the service operates through 24×7 state control rooms and district Child Helpline units.
• Emergency 112: Use for emergency/police assistance.
• Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015: A key legal framework for care, protection, rehabilitation and child-friendly processes.
• Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (POCSO): Key law protecting children from sexual offences.
• Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006: Provides the legal framework for prohibition of child marriage and related offences.
• Mission Vatsalya: Includes child protection structures and services such as Child Welfare Committees, Juvenile Justice Boards, District Child Protection Units, emergency outreach and family-based care.
• Cyber Crime Reporting: The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal has a special focus on cyber crimes against women and children. Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930.

Hindi — KAWACH में इस्तेमाल होने वाले आधिकारिक स्रोत:
• Child Helpline 1098: Mission Vatsalya के तहत Child Helpline राज्य/जिला तंत्र के साथ संचालित होती है और ERSS-112 से integrated है। आधिकारिक Ministry page के अनुसार State और District level पर 24×7 व्यवस्था उपलब्ध है।
• Emergency 112: आपातकालीन/पुलिस सहायता के लिए।
• Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015: बच्चों की care, protection, rehabilitation और child-friendly processes के लिए प्रमुख कानूनी framework।
• POCSO Act, 2012: बच्चों को sexual offences से protection देने वाला प्रमुख कानून।
• Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006: बाल विवाह की रोकथाम और संबंधित अपराधों के लिए कानूनी framework।
• Mission Vatsalya: Child Welfare Committees, Juvenile Justice Boards, District Child Protection Units, emergency outreach और family-based care जैसी child-protection services/structures को शामिल करता है।
• Cyber Crime Reporting: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal पर women/children से संबंधित cyber crime reporting की सुविधा है। Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930।
